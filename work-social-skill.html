Social Media Manager — an agent skill

An agent skill that runs social media end to end: it finds which connectors actually work, keeps a knowledge base of your brand, then plans, writes, humanises, schedules and reports.

What it is

On first use, whatever you type, it does the same first step: it lists the connectors available, tests real access, and tells you plainly which platforms are ready and which need connecting. Then it asks about your topic, audience and cadence, studies your existing posts, and starts planning.

What is in the build

  • Six knowledge templates you fill in once: brand voice, platforms, content pillars, past posts, cadence, ingest log. Every post after that sounds like you, not like a model.
  • Four references: connector discovery, the research-to-report workflow, a per-platform playbook, and how the knowledge base is maintained.
  • Fifteen platforms covered, including LinkedIn, Instagram, X, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Reddit and Discord — and an honest answer when one is not available.
  • Connector-agnostic: it works with whatever your agent has, native or through a scheduler such as Buffer, Metricool or Postiz.

What it taught me

Three design rules carry the whole thing: never publish without explicit approval, never fabricate metrics, never store credentials. Everything else is workflow. MIT licensed, so it can be forked and made better.
