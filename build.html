Five things I do before I trust anything an AI built.

Not gates for a launch, gates for my own conscience. Skip one and the work goes back in the drawer.

01

Describe the job until guessing is impossible

Role, situation, constraint, and exactly what should come back. If I cannot describe it, the AI fills the gap with invention, and the fault is mine, not the model's.

02

Hand over the material, not just the words

The thumbnails that got clicks, the real posts, the live numbers through a connector. Context is what stops it designing for a customer who does not exist.

03

Give it a loop with a stop condition

Repeat until the condition is met, not until it feels finished. Write the condition down before you start, or you will accept whatever comes back.

04

Put the examiner outside the loop

A separate step with its own rules, that the writer cannot instruct, and a human holding the final call. A writer that grades its own work grades itself generously.

05

Keep the notes

What worked goes back into the repo so the next run starts ahead of the last one, and nothing ships that I did not read myself.
